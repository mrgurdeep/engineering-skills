# cr-analysis · Step 3/11 — Extract requirements and acceptance criteria

**Inputs:** snapshot, `HUB/src/templates/requirements.md`

**Do:**
1. Goals, success metrics, roles, in/out of scope, dependencies, assumptions, milestones.
2. `FR-n` as user stories with MoSCoW priority; `AC-n.m` in Given/When/Then (≥1 per FR); `NFR-*` with targets; inferred edge cases marked *inferred*.
3. Open questions `# | question | why | owner | blocking?`; traceability seed `FR | AC | component | story | test`.

**Output:** `01-requirements.md`

**Checkpoint ⏸ (wait for the user):** User approved requirements or answered blocking questions.
